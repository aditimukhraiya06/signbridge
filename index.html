Sign Your Way

Communication without barriers.

One accessibility assistant for communication, learning, emergency support and navigation.

What works today

Hand tracking runs on your camera. Letters: ASL uses finger-position rules and ISL uses built-in two-hand samples; both improve when you teach your own signs in Communicate → Teach. Words: a small trained model plus any words you record yourself (this is how movement words like help and cry work best).

ASL and ISL each keep their own samples.

🧠 Teach the app your signs

Start the camera, pick a letter, press Record and hold that sign steady while tilting your hand slightly. Repeat for each letter. Samples stay on this device, separately for ASL and ISL.

Teach a word (with movement)

Start the camera, type a word, press Record, then sign it fully. Do it about 3 times per word. Works for movement words like help or cry.

📷 How to hold your hand up to the camera
  1. Sit about an arm's length from the screen.
  2. Keep the whole hand (and both hands for ISL) inside the frame.
  3. Use a plain background and light in front of you.
  4. Hold the sign still for about a second.

🤟 Ask with a sign

Sign a letter (or a word, if you installed a word model) and Sign Your Way asks to find that place. Letters: T toilet, H hospital, P pharmacy, F food, B bus, A ATM, S police. Teach those letters first in Communicate. You can also speak, or tap the buttons below.
