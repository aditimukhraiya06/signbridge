Sign Your Way

Communication without barriers.

One accessibility assistant for communication, learning, emergency support and navigation.

What works today

Hand tracking runs on your camera. Letters: teach the app your own hand shapes (A–Z) in Communicate → Teach. Words: switch to Words mode after you train a model with the Colab notebook and upload the models/ folder. No model means no words; nothing is faked.

ASL and ISL each have their own samples and their own model.

🤟 Ask with a sign

Sign a letter (or a word, if you installed a word model) and Sign Your Way asks to find that place. Letters: T toilet, H hospital, P pharmacy, F food, B bus, A ATM, S police. Teach those letters first in Communicate. You can also speak, or tap the buttons below.
